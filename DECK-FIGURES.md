so the two columns describe the same set of questions. An unpaired average

## The capability finding

- **Enhanced** answered **5 of 6** availability questions.
- **Standard** answered **0 of 6** availability questions.

This is the headline. It is a capability difference, not a quality
difference: the standard arm cannot answer these at all, which is also
why its RAGAS scores on that row are 0.0000 rather than merely low.
